[00:00 - 00:45] Hinjewadi Infrastructure & Location:
Welcome to Pride Purple Park Titan, the landmark residential development in Hinjewadi Phase 1, Pune. Situated strategically on the Hinjewadi-Marunji Link Road, Park Titan places you just 2 minutes from Wipro Circle, 3 minutes from the upcoming Hinjewadi–Shivajinagar Metro Line 3 station, and minutes from Infosys, TCS, and Quadron Business Park. Say goodbye to long daily commutes and reclaim quality family time.

Park Titan spans an expansive 17+ acres, anchored by an unprecedented 7-acre vehicle-free podium. Here, children play freely without traffic hazards, seniors enjoy serene oxygen parks, and fitness enthusiasts access world-class jogging circuits surrounded by lush Miyawaki urban forest landscaping.

Professional coaching is delivered directly inside your residential gates.
