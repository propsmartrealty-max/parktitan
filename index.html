Hinjawadi, Pune — Where The City Is Growing

Hinjawadi is Pune's largest IT and business corridor. The Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park alone is home to Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, TCS, Tech Mahindra, Capgemini and KPIT.

Pune's growth continues to move west. The upcoming Hinjawadi–Shivajinagar Metro, the Pune Ring Road and direct access to the Mumbai–Pune Expressway are shortening the distance between Hinjawadi and the rest of the city.

Park Titan sits in Marunji, on the Hinjawadi–Marunji Link Road — inside the corridor and close to Phase 1.
