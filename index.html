Site Plan & Master Layout

A Township Planned Around How You Live.

Spanning over 17 expansive acres on the prominent Hinjewadi-Marunji Link Road, Park Titan exemplifies thoughtful macro-urban planning. Rather than overcrowding the parcel with dense, inward-facing concrete blocks, Pride Purple Group prioritized an open-horizon blueprint where 70% of the entire land area is preserved as lush landscaped greens, pedestrian avenues, and active recreational facilities.

The core of the township is anchored by an extraordinary 7-acre vehicle-free podium deck. By channelling all vehicular entry, service trucks, and resident parking directly to subterranean levels and outer perimeter corridors, the podium functions as an elevated sanctuary. Here, young children, morning runners, and senior citizens can move about with complete freedom, total safety, and zero traffic fumes.

The 18 residential towers are positioned in staggered alignments to capture the prevailing winds that sweep across the Sahyadri foothills, maximising natural cross ventilation and ambient daylighting in every home. Low core density with only six apartments per floor ensures that your home life remains peaceful, exclusive, and connected to nature.
