Architectural & Engineering Specifications

Technical reference guide for Pride Purple Park Titan Hinjewadi. Documenting structural engineering standards, seismic safety, vehicle-free podium architecture, on-site sports academies, and statutory MahaRERA compliance.

Structural Engineering

Mivan Monolithic RCC Construction

100% cast-in-place monolithic concrete walls and slabs utilizing precision aluminum formwork. Eliminates brick masonry joints, guarantees uniform wall finishes, accelerates completion velocity, and provides superior seismic resilience in Zone III.

Master Layout

7-Acre Vehicle-Free Active Podium

Western Pune's largest continuous elevated podium deck. By routing 100% of vehicular traffic and parking into dedicated basement levels, the podium deck provides a safe, serene pedestrian sanctuary for children and senior citizens.

Density & Privacy

Low-Density Core Architecture

Floor-plate engineering restricted to strictly 6 residences per core across G+23 floors. Each residential tower is served by 3 high-speed passenger and stretcher elevators to eliminate peak-hour wait times.

Sports Academy #1

Padukone School of Badminton

On-site national badminton academy founded by Prakash Padukone. Offers professional-grade indoor courts, structured training batches, and certified coaching staff for junior and adult residents.

Sports Academy #2

BSA Bluewater Swim Academy

Professional aquatic training managed by Olympian coach Arjun Muralidharan. Features an Olympic-length pool with heated junior splash zones and competitive swim coaching.

Performing Arts #1

Shiamak Davar Dance Academy

Premier modern, contemporary, and Bollywood dance academy operating out of the Park Titan cultural studio. Offers structured weekend and weekday classes.

Performing Arts #2

Suresh Wadkar Ajivasan Music Academy

Comprehensive classical and contemporary music training facility with vocal rooms, acoustic recording equipment, and instrumental training under maestro Suresh Wadkar's methodology.

Junior Sports Academy

International early childhood sports academy focusing on gross motor skill development, team coordination, and foundational training in football, tennis, and athletics for children aged 2.5 to 8 years.

Statutory Certification

MahaRERA Sanctions & Compliance

Officially registered and sanctioned across multiple development phases under the Real Estate Regulatory Authority: Phase 1 (P52100028049), Phase 2 (P52100030567), and Phase 5 (P52100047702). Full documents verified on maharerait.maharashtra.gov.in.

Transit Catalyst

PMRDA Metro Line 3 Connectivity

The upcoming 23-station elevated Pune Metro Line 3 connects Hinjewadi Phase 1 directly to Shivajinagar in 35 minutes. Park Titan is located just 1.0 km from the nearest metro station along the Hinjewadi-Marunji Link Road.
