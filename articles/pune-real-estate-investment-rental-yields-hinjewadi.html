Why Hinjewadi Outperforms Wakad, Baner & Kharadi in Rental Yields

1. Macroeconomic Drivers of Pune's Real Estate Market

Pune has emerged as India’s top tier-2 city for technology investment, automotive R&D, and global capability centers (GCCs). Unlike Mumbai, where astronomical land valuations compress gross rental yields to 2.2%–2.8%, Pune maintains strong fundamentals, generating gross rental yields between 3.5% and 5.4%.

Within Pune, however, rental yield performance varies dramatically by locality. While prime mature sectors like Koregaon Park, Kalyani Nagar, and Baner deliver sub-3.5% yields due to high acquisition costs, the IT corridor of Hinjewadi stands as Pune’s highest rental yield generator.
