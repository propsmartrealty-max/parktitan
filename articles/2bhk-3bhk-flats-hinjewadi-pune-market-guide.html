1. The Western Pune Residential Expansion: Hinjewadi, Mahalunge & Baner

3. The Architectural Differentiator: Zero-Wastage Carpet Area vs Super Built-Up

One of the biggest pitfalls Pune homebuyers encounter is the 'loading factor'. Standalone buildings in Wakad and Mahalunge frequently impose 35% to 42% loading over the carpet area, meaning buyers pay heavily for common stairwells, passages, and duct spaces.

At Pride Purple Park Titan Hinjewadi, the design philosophy is built upon zero-wastage functional ergonomics:

  • 2 BHK Optima (726 sq.ft.) provides an expansive 10'6" × 18' living-dining zone with a private balcony, an en-suite master bedroom, and an attached dry balcony.
  • 2 BHK Ultima (789 sq.ft.) introduces architectural bay windows that capture uninterrupted natural daylight while creating cozy reading nooks.
  • 3 BHK Grande (1,212 sq.ft.) rivals 4 BHK apartments in Baner in terms of living room breadth and master suite privacy.
