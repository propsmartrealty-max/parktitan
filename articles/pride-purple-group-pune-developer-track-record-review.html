Why Monolithic Mivan Formwork Matters

Traditional brick-and-mortar masonry construction frequently suffers from uneven wall plaster, hairline joint fissures, moisture seepage, and slow construction cycles. At Park Titan Hinjewadi, Pride Purple deployed precision aluminum formwork (Mivan RCC technology):

Engineering Metric Mivan Monolithic RCC (Park Titan) Conventional Brick Masonry
Structural Jointing Monolithic concrete pour (Zero mortar joints) Prone to brick-mortar separation & cracks
Seismic Performance Superior shear wall resistance (Zone 3 engineered) Vulnerable infill brick movement during tremors
Moisture Barrier Impermeable high-density concrete skin High porosity leading to dampness and paint peel
Carpet Space Efficiency Slimmer, high-strength walls (Maximum usable sq.ft.) Thick 9-inch brickwork eating into carpet area
Surface Precision Mirror-smooth aluminum casting finish Dependent on manual trowel plastering consistency

The 7-Acre Raised Podium Engineering Triumph

At Park Titan, Pride Purple engineered West Pune's largest 7-acre continuous active podium deck, elevated 25 feet above the ground. Below the podium lies dedicated multi-level vehicular circulation and parking, completely isolating children, joggers, and seniors from vehicle traffic above. On top of this deck sit 5 national sports academies and 40+ curated resort amenities.
