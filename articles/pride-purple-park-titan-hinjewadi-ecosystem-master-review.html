Pride Purple Park Titan Hinjewadi:
Master Layout, Architectural Features & In-Depth Review

1. Project Overview: The Crown Flagship of Park District

Spanning over 17 acres of prime land along the Hinjewadi-Marunji Link Road, Park Titan represents Pride Purple Group's most ambitious residential undertaking to date. Conceived as the premier residential cluster within the larger 60-acre Park District integrated township, the project blends high-density IT connectivity with low-density sanctuary living.

Comprising 18 high-rise architectural towers rising G+23 floors, each tower floorplate is configured with only 6 apartments per floor serviced by three high-speed passenger elevators and two dedicated fire escape staircases.

2. Master Layout & Spatial Zoning

The master site plan of Park Titan is anchored around a central, raised 7-acre podium that links all 18 residential towers. Key zones include:

  • The Central Aquatics Hub: Semi-Olympic lap pool, children's splash pool, submerged loungers, and poolside cabanas.
  • The Sports Pavilion: Professional indoor badminton courts, squash court, tennis courts, and basketball arena.
  • Wellness & Social Enclaves: 1-kilometer continuous jogging track, acupressure pathways, meditation lawn, and open-air amphitheater.
  • The Academy Center: Dedicated spaces for the five on-site academies, providing professional coaching right inside the township gates.

3. MahaRERA Certifications & Regulatory Transparency

Compliance and consumer protection are strictly maintained under MahaRERA regulations:

  • Registration Numbers: P52100049631, P52100080502, P52100031768, and P52100034961.

  • Title Clearances: Encumbrance-free title held by Pride Purple Infrastructure / Purple Corp Pvt Ltd.
