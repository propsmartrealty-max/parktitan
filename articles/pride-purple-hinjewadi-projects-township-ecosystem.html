Pride Purple Hinjewadi Projects: Complete Master Township Ecosystem Guide

Published by Pride Purple Market Intelligence Desk · Updated October 2026 · 8 Min Read

Over the past decade, Hinjewadi has evolved from a rural agricultural tract into Maharashtra's foremost technology powerhouse, housing more than 400,000 IT professionals across 3 sprawling phases of the Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park. At the center of this transformation stands Pride Purple Group, whose master-planned developments on the Hinjewadi-Marunji Link Road have redefined community density, pedestrian safety, and long-term asset value.

The 60-Acre Park District Ecosystem

Unlike standalone towers crammed into narrow arterial roads, Pride Purple conceived the Park District as an integrated 60-acre master township. Positioned strategically on the Hinjewadi-Marunji Link Road, just 0.8 km from Wipro Circle, this township decouples residential tranquility from vehicular congestion.

  • Park Titan (Flagship Phase): 17+ acres comprising 18 high-rise towers, Pune's largest 7-acre vehicle-free active podium, and 5 nationally certified sports academies.
  • Park Astra: Contemporary 2 & 3 BHK high-rise residences emphasizing green buffers and family sports infrastructure.
  • Park Connect: High-efficiency compact luxury homes tailored for young technology executives seeking immediate proximity to Phase 1 campuses.

The Park Titan Flagship Advantage

Within this ecosystem, Park Titan commands flagship status. Built with monolithic aluminum formwork (Mivan RCC technology), the 18 towers rise 23 stories above an elevated 7-acre podium deck. This architectural design completely eliminates vehicular movement from the pedestrian recreation level, allowing children and senior citizens to enjoy 40+ lifestyle amenities with zero safety hazards.
